### Overall Impression
The portfolio is a masterclass in restraint and typographic hierarchy. It successfully executes the "engineering dossier" aesthetic, feeling premium, focused, and highly professional. The single biggest opportunity is to ensure that the rigorous minimalism doesn't compromise accessibility, particularly regarding contrast and touch targets on mobile.

### What's Working
1. **Brutal Consistency and Restraint**: The strict adherence to a single 1px translucent hairline for boundaries and a single accent color creates an incredibly cohesive, premium, and distraction-free experience.
2. **Typographic Hierarchy**: The deliberate pairing of expanded Archivo for bold declarations and JetBrains Mono for quiet, factual data perfectly reinforces the "evidence over ornament" philosophy.
3. **Person-First Hero**: The soft-masked portrait (`.hero-mask`) gracefully blends the image into the dark canvas, establishing immediate personal connection without rigid borders.

### Priority Issues

- **[P2] Text Contrast on Data Elements**
  - **Why it matters**: The `--color-mute` text (`#9e9a94`) on the `--color-paper` background (`#1f1b17`) has a contrast ratio of ~4.54:1. While this just passes WCAG AA guidelines, it may strain readability for users with mild vision impairments, especially on lower-brightness screens.
  - **Fix**: Lighten `--color-mute` slightly to increase the contrast ratio while maintaining its secondary position in the visual hierarchy.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable colorize`

- **[P2] Mobile Headline Wrapping in Contact Section**
  - **Why it matters**: The display headline ("Have a backend worth building? Let's talk.") is set to clamp up to 4.5rem on a 16ch measure. On very narrow viewports (<360px), this aggressive scaling might result in awkward line breaks or jagged orphans, disrupting the aesthetic close.
  - **Fix**: Adjust the lower bound of the clamp or rely more heavily on `text-wrap: balance` to ensure graceful wrapping on the smallest screens.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable typeset`

- **[P3] Interactive Touch Targets on Mobile**
  - **Why it matters**: The inline `react-icons` links in the Projects section ("View on GitHub") are styled elegantly as text but may lack the optimal 44x44px touch target area required for comfortable mobile interaction.
  - **Fix**: Add invisible padding (e.g., `py-2`) to these inline links to increase their touchable footprint without altering their visual design.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable adapt`

### Persona Red Flags

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent User)**:
- While the HTML structure is mostly sound, the borderline contrast on `.data` elements (dates, stacks, locations) might pose a challenge if Sam has low vision. The reliance on purely visual hover states for the `MagicPill` and custom cursor provides no equivalent experience for keyboard-only or screen-reader users.

**Casey (Distracted Mobile User)**:
- Casey might struggle to tap the inline "View on GitHub" links in the Projects section if they are rushing or using the phone one-handed, due to the potentially small touch target size of bare inline links.
